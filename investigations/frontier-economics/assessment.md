## Competing interpretations, assumptions and invalidation

These are **authored conditional hypotheses by issue-6-engineer**, not observed conclusions about actual margins, subsidies or capability. All three assessments cite the same retained financial baseline and separately named missing inputs; evidence does not satisfy their missing premises. `verification=source_checked` records text/byte checking only, not independent financial verification, and global coverage/freshness-of-financial-reality remain null. ### A. Diversified-partner buffer — `assessment:issue6:buffer:1`

- **Interpretation:** positive retained-demand unit contribution plus available partner cash could sustain frontier investment even before each training cycle pays back. This is a possibility, not an assertion that those conditions hold.
- **Assumptions:** corporate revenue converts to available cash; AI cash demand does not exhaust that cash; workload retention and realized receipts support positive serving contribution; capital allocation and contractual terms permit continued investment. None is measured here.
- **Strongest available counterevidence/limit:** the entire numeric baseline is corporate-wide secondary revenue, while AI allocation, cash conversion, retention, training outlay and payment schedule are null. Preferred rank and repeated issuer-linked reporting do not rescue these premises. No observed negative margin is asserted.
- **Observable invalidation:** qualify a same-entity, same-period cash/payment schedule and retained-demand cohort showing available unrestricted cash plus completed financing is insufficient for payable obligations, or sustained realized unit contribution is negative after fully loaded serving cost. A corporate revenue total alone cannot pass or invalidate this hypothesis.

### B. Financing-dependent fragility — `assessment:issue6:fragility:1`

- **Interpretation:** if realized paid demand does not cover serving/training outlay and payable compute obligations without new finance, apparent expansion could mask fragile cash economics. No current loss, subsidy or bubble is declared.
- **Assumptions:** unfavorable retained-demand contribution; material cash obligations distinct from announcements; limited ability to reduce/restructure spending; financing not guaranteed. All remain unknown.
- **Strongest available counterevidence:** the acquired secondary revenue baseline supplies a concrete alternative to “no economic activity”; diversified partners could provide a buffer under A’s unmeasured premises. This is a source-backed reason not to equate absent private numbers with a proved collapse. There is no direct measured counterexample for private-lab fragility in this pack.
- **Observable invalidation:** obtain qualified retained-customer cohorts with positive realized contribution plus same-period completed cash receipts/available liquidity sufficient for training outlay and contractual payments without uncommitted future funding. Announced rounds, run rates, cloud credits or a promised IPO alone do not invalidate B.

### C. Value migrates to substitutes — `assessment:issue6:substitutes:1`

- **Interpretation:** if a legally usable open substitute meets equivalent workload/service requirements at lower fully loaded cost, frontier price compression could move value toward users/infrastructure unless frontier cost reduction or differentiated retention compensates.
- **Assumptions:** comparable quality/latency/reliability and deployment rights; lower end-to-end cost rather than only token list price; adoption and switching feasible; frontier differentiation/cost improvement insufficient. All remain unknown; no distillation origin or illegality is presumed.
- **Strongest available counterevidence/limit:** the admitted corporate revenue baseline is not lab margin or substitute performance; no comparable model/cost experiment or retained-price series exists in licensed inputs. Thus this pack cannot claim commoditization has occurred. A/B are plausible competing mechanisms, not measurements defeating C.
- **Observable invalidation:** same-workload qualified repeated comparisons show the substitute fails required quality/service constraints or loses its cost advantage, or licensed realized price/cohort/cost evidence shows durable frontier contribution and differentiated retention despite available comparable substitutes. A leaderboard or open licence alone cannot decide it.

A/B/C can coexist under different conditions; no unsupported logical `contradicts` edge is manufactured. Qualifying a new input appends a new source/claim/assessment revision and records which premise changed; it does not silently replace old evidence or convert nulls to estimates.
