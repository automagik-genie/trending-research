Locations: brain.py 143–153,302–318; inner 31,74,115–127; outer 47–56,62–70,88–95.
In the BrainEvidence constructor, except BaseException invokes self.close() before bare raise. If close itself raises (nonzero child exit, forced termination, failed stdin closure, unfinished reader), it replaces the original NativeError/decode exception. The caller never receives a constructed client, so inner client stays None and cannot recover its calls/redactions.
Therefore an actual initialize/tools-list error whose hash/safe fields were already created at 183–185 can be reduced to only a cleanup exception type by inner 115–119.
For a later native error, inner stores result fields in memory, then executes client.close() before its single stdout print. A blocked close or native-phase cutoff causes outer to kill the child; the outer ignores any partial stdout on TimeoutExpired and uses its initial result, losing the newly captured causal/error/attempt data.
This is separate from preserving the historical 61649 old calls: the atomic copy protects those, but does not recover the diagnostic that died before publication.
Impact: transport-failure custody does not meet the promised capture-before-cleanup evidence preservation; an original failure can be masked by its cleanup failure, and the new diagnosis can vanish.
Correction: preserve the primary error's bounded receipt separately from cleanup outcome; make cleanup failure additive, not replacement.
