AI research trend tracker ? Scores are 0–100 signal scores (scoring v2), never star totals. Full formulas: Methodology tab.
Each present signal is log-scaled against the run-wide P95 (one outlier can't squash the rest), combined with the weights below over the signals present, then shrunk toward the median by confidence (share of evidence present), so thin evidence can't post extreme scores.
Repos: star growth/day 25% · issue activity 15% · PR volume 10% · merge rate 10% · commits 15% · contributors 10% · contributor growth 15%. Star spikes without matching issue/PR/commit activity are capped, solo repos and one-off spikes are discounted.
Papers: HF upvote momentum 40% · linked-repo trust 35% · HN mention momentum 25%. Momentum = real deltas between our snapshots when we have them, else per-day-since-release (~ = rate proxy).
Arrows compare ranks only among items in both runs and ignore tiny changes. — = no data.
